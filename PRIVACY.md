# Privacy Policy — Midnight Horde

Last updated: 2026-10-31 · Developer: Moonhorde Games · Contact: moonhordegames@proton.me

**Short version:** Midnight Horde is a paid game. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics. We do not collect, share or sell any personal data.

**What the game stores on your device.** Your progress, settings and nickname are saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we do not run any servers.

**Nickname.** The nickname you choose is shown only inside the game on your device.

**Time check.** To stop clock manipulation, the game makes a simple HTTPS request to a public Google endpoint and reads the date in the response header. No personal data is sent.

**Advertising, tracking and analytics.** The game contains no advertising, no advertising ID usage, no third-party analytics and no tracking. Google Play may provide us anonymous crash and performance data through the Play Console.

**Purchases.** The game itself is bought on Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details.

**Children.** The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

**Your choices.** You can delete all local data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
