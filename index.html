Privacy Policy – Midnight Horde

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy applies to the mobile game "Midnight Horde" (package com.moonhorde.midnighthorde) by Moonhorde Games ("we").

1. Data we collect

We do not run any server, account system or analytics service, and we do not collect, sell or share personal data ourselves. Your progress, settings and language choice are stored only on your device, in the game's own save file. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: interstitial ads between rounds and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process certain data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and ad interactions. We do not receive this data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more in how Google uses data when you use partner apps and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact

Moonhorde Games – moonhordegames@proton.me
