Privacy Policy — Midnight Horde

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Developer: Moonhorde Games · Contact: moonhordegames@proton.me
Short version: Midnight Horde is a paid game. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics. We do not collect, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings and nickname are saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. We do not run any servers.

Nickname

The nickname you choose is shown only inside the game, on your device.

Google Play

The game is distributed and paid for through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google, and we never receive your payment details. The game does not use Google Play Games sign-in, cloud saves, leaderboards or any other account service. Google may provide us anonymous crash and performance statistics through the Google Play Console; these contain no personal data that identifies you.

Time check

To stop clock manipulation, the game makes a simple HTTPS request to a public Google endpoint and reads the date in the response header. No personal data is sent.

Advertising, tracking and analytics

The game contains no advertising, does not use your advertising ID, and includes no third-party analytics or tracking.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your choices

You can delete all local game data at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
