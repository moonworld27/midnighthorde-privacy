Privacy Policy — Rallyborn Survivors: Premium

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · App: Rallyborn Survivors: Premium (com.moonhorde.midnighthorde) · Developer: Moonhorde Games · Contact: moonhordegames@proton.me
Short version: Rallyborn Survivors: Premium has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts of our own and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device. We do not run any servers, and we do not collect, share or sell your personal data. Two optional Google features (Google Play Games and the in-app review prompt) and one optional reminder notification are described below.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings and nickname are saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. We do not run any servers.

Nickname

The nickname you choose is shown inside the game, on your device. It is not uploaded to us.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Purchase and payment are handled entirely by Google, and we never receive your payment details. Through the Google Play Console, Google may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and performance statistics; these do not identify you.

Google Play Games (optional)

The game can offer optional sign-in with Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. Playing without signing in is fully possible. If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player ID and display name) together with the scores and achievements the game submits, under Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games terms. We do not receive a copy of your Google account details, and we do not use this information for anything else. You can sign out or manage your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app and in your Google account settings.

Reminder notification (optional)

If you allow notifications (Android 13 and later asks for permission), the game can schedule a local reminder on your device, for example when a daily reward is ready. The reminder is created and shown by your device; no notification data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn it off at any time in the game settings or in Android's notification settings.

In-app review (optional)

The game may occasionally ask whether you would like to rate it. The prompt is provided by Google Play's in-app review service. Whether you rate, and what you write, is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the game does not receive your review or any personal data from it.

Time check

To stop clock manipulation, the game makes a simple HTTPS request to a public Google endpoint (google.com) and reads the date in the response header. This request carries no account or personal data, but like any internet request it is visible to the server and to your network operator (for example your IP address).

Advertising, tracking and analytics

The game contains no advertising, does not use your advertising ID, and includes no third-party analytics, tracking or attribution tools.

Permissions

The game uses internet access (the time check and the optional Google features above), vibration (haptic feedback), and, only if you allow it, notifications (the optional reminder). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your choices and data deletion

You can delete all local game data at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings. Because we hold no copy of your game data, there is nothing further for us to delete. For Google Play Games data, use the Play Games app or your Google account settings. If you have any question or request, write to moonhordegames@proton.me.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
