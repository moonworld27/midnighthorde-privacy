Privacy Policy – Midnight Horde

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy applies to the mobile game "Midnight Horde" (package com.moonhorde.midnighthorde) by Moonhorde Games ("we").

1. Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. The game has no account system, no server of ours and no analytics SDK. Your progress, settings and language choice are stored only on your device, in the game's own save file. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

2. In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed.

3. Third parties

The game does not include advertising or tracking services. Google Play itself may process data under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact

Moonhorde Games – moonhordegames@proton.me
